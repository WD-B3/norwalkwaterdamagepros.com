Emergency Water Heater Leak Cleanup in Norwalk, OH

A tank that fails can release 40 gallons or more. Learn how to shut it down and how the cleanup and drying work.

☎ (567) 406-9153See all services

A water heater is a pressurized tank that usually holds 40 to 50 gallons, and when its steel liner corrodes through, most of that water can end up on the floor. Because the tank sits in a basement corner or a utility room, the leak often runs for a while before anyone sees it.

This page is about the water heater as the source. The steps start with shutting the unit down safely, then extracting water, drying the floor and walls and checking materials near the tank, including drywall and flooring.

When a water heater leak is an emergency

A steady stream, a hissing sound with steam or a puddle spreading across a floor is an emergency. Close the cold water supply valve above the tank. Then switch off the electric breaker for an electric heater, or turn a gas unit's control to its pilot or off position. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first.

Do not step into standing water around an electric heater until the breaker is off and the panel is dry. The phone is answered at all hours, and a crew can be sent that day when one is free. Say whether the heater is gas or electric when you call, because the crew's safety steps differ.

Cold supply valve

Close it on the pipe entering the top of the tank.

Power or gas

Breaker off for electric, pilot or off for gas.

Drain hose

Only drain the tank if the leak is at a fitting and you can attach a hose to the valve.

Contents

Move boxes and stored items off the floor.

Why tanks fail

Steel tanks corrode from the inside once the glass-like liner and the sacrificial anode rod wear down. Sediment, high water temperature and age all shorten tank life. A leak at the pressure relief valve or a fitting is often repairable, while a leak from the tank body means replacement.

Norwalk's city-owned water system supplies the line that feeds the heater. If you notice rust-colored water or sudden pressure changes, the city's utility office can tell you whether anything changed on the supply side, which helps your plumber diagnose the cause.

What gets wet around a water heater

  • Subfloor and flooring, especially vinyl, laminate and engineered wood
  • The lower section of drywall and baseboards
  • Stored boxes, cardboard and fabric
  • The furnace and other equipment sharing the same room, which should be checked by a technician before it is turned back on

Basement tanks put water on a concrete floor, which drains toward the lowest point. The basement water damage cleanup page covers rooms with a finished ceiling and carpet. If the leak reaches a finished floor above, see the hardwood floor page for wood repairs.

A failed tank empties fast, so the quicker the valve closes, the smaller the job.

How cleanup and drying work

A technician extracts standing water, then uses a moisture meter to map wet drywall and baseboards. Wet baseboards are removed so the wall bottom can breathe. Air movers and a dehumidifier dry the room until readings fall back to normal, usually over a few days. Water-heater water is usually Category 1 when fresh, but it can turn contaminated if it sits on a dirty floor for long, so speed matters.

Plumbing replacement, including a new tank or a drain pan with a drain line, belongs to a licensed plumber. Ask the plumber about a drain pan and a leak alarm when the new tank goes in.

What affects water heater leak cleanup cost

The size of the job tracks how far the water traveled:

  • How many gallons were released before shutoff
  • Flooring type and whether it must be removed
  • Whether drywall and baseboards were soaked
  • Equipment days
  • Whether the heater sits above a finished room

Sudden tank failure is often covered under homeowners policies, while a long-running slow leak often is not. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and keep the failed tank until the adjuster has seen it. Want your real number? Call dispatch.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup questions in Norwalk, OH

Do you handle Water Heater Leak Cleanup emergencies at night or on weekends in Norwalk?

Yes. Tanks fail at night as often as in the day, so dispatch is open every hour. Crews go out the same day when one is free. Close the cold water supply valve on top of the heater, switch off its electric breaker or set a gas unit to pilot or off, and keep people out of standing water until the power is confirmed off.

Should I drain a leaking water heater myself?

If the leak is from a fitting and you can safely reach the drain valve, draining can reduce the water on the floor. If the tank body is leaking, shut off the supply and power and call. Never work near an electric heater with water on the floor.

Is water heater water contaminated?

Fresh tank water is usually clean, but if it sits on a dirty floor for more than a day it can pick up contaminants. That is why extraction is done promptly.

Can a water heater leak cause mold?

If materials around it stay damp, yes. EPA guidance says to dry water-damaged materials within 24 to 48 hours, which is why the area is dried with equipment and not just mopped.
