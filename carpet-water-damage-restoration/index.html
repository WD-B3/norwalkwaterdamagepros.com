Carpet Water Damage in Norwalk, OH

Carpet and pad hold water like a sponge. Here is how to decide whether to dry or replace them.

☎ (567) 406-9153See all services

Carpet is made of fibers over a backing, and below that sits a foam pad that can hold many gallons of water. A wet carpet can feel only slightly damp on top while the pad and the subfloor underneath stay soaked. That is why wet carpet is a bigger job than it looks.

This page covers wet carpet and pad. Hard flooring has its own page for hardwood floors, and standing water on top of carpet begins with water extraction.

Can wet carpet be saved?

The answer depends on the water category and the time. Clean water caught within a day or so often lets carpet be dried in place, and the pad is the first thing to replace. Contaminated water, from a sewer or a flood, means carpet and pad are normally discarded.

The rule of thumb in ANSI/IICRC S500 is that Category 3 water means porous materials are removed. Ask any company whether its crews work to S500.

How wet carpet is dried

Technicians lift part of the carpet at a wall, remove the pad and dry the subfloor and the carpet backing with air movers and a dehumidifier. This method is called floating the carpet. A moisture meter checks the subfloor, and the carpet is put back after the pad is replaced.

  • Water is extracted from the carpet with a weighted extractor
  • Pad is removed and bagged
  • Carpet is raised on air movers so air flows under it
  • The subfloor is dried and measured
  • Carpet is cleaned and sanitized before it is laid back

What goes wrong when carpet stays wet

Carpet that stays wet for more than a couple of days can develop a sour smell, show browning from wicking stains and grow mold in the pad. EPA guidance says to dry water-damaged materials within 24 to 48 hours, and a pad that is not dry is the usual start of mold in carpeted rooms.

If mold is already visible, the mold remediation page explains what testing and removal involve, and health questions go to your physician.

Wet pad dries slowly, so a quick call usually means more of the carpet is saved.

Carpet in Norwalk basements and living areas

Carpeted basements are common in homes, and water from rain or a sump overflow is rarely clean. The basement page covers that case. In upper floors, water from ceilings or appliances is often clean, so drying in place is more realistic.

Norwalk's NOAA normals list about 33 inches of snow a year, and melting snow tracked in on boots can soak entry carpets as well. The practical step is to keep mats at doors and to dry them promptly.

What affects carpet water damage cost

Carpet jobs are scoped by room:

  • Square footage of carpet and pad affected
  • Water category
  • Whether the carpet is saved or replaced
  • Drying days for the subfloor
  • Cleaning or deodorizing
  • Replacement carpet and pad

Sudden water from a failed line is often covered, while repeated seepage is often not. Check your policy or ask your insurer. Want your real number? Call dispatch.

Questions to settle before work starts

  • Is the water clean, gray or black
  • How long has the carpet been wet
  • Is the pad foam, rebond or something else
  • Is there a moisture problem in the subfloor

Those four answers usually decide whether the carpet is dried in place or pulled up. Ask them on the phone and be ready to describe what you see.

Carpet Water Damage questions in Norwalk, OH

How do I know if my carpet pad is wet?

Press the carpet with a dry towel and a hand. If water squishes up or the carpet feels cold and heavy, the pad is wet. A moisture meter confirms it.

Will wet carpet smell even after drying?

If it is dried quickly, usually not. A lingering sour smell suggests the pad or subfloor stayed wet and needs another look.

Can I rent a carpet cleaner to dry it?

A rented extractor removes surface water but does not dry the pad or subfloor. Without dehumidifiers and air movers, hidden moisture often remains.
