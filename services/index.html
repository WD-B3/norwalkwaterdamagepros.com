Every water damage job is different, so the pages below are organized by the source of the water or the part of the building it reached. Start with the page that matches what you see. If you are not sure, call dispatch and describe it. The homepage explains the first 72 hours in general terms.

Mold is listed last on purpose. It is usually a result of unresolved moisture, so the water pages come first and the mold page explains what follows when drying is late. Every page names the safety steps to take before help arrives and the cost factors that shape the bill.
