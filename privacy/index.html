Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you call or visit this website.

☎ (567) 406-9153See all services

This page explains how Norwalk Water Damage Pros handles information when you use this website or call the dispatch line. It is written in plain language, and it applies to norwalkwaterdamagepros.com.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may ask for your name, address, phone number and a description of the damage so dispatch can send the right help.

This website

This website has no forms, no accounts and no comment sections. Our hosting provider and the fonts and map services we use may process technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages.

How we use information

  • To respond to your call and send help
  • To improve how dispatch answers questions
  • To meet legal or insurance record requirements

Sharing

We share the details of a job with the restoration professionals sent to your address, and with your insurer if you ask us to. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where the law allows, by emailing contact@norwalkwaterdamagepros.com or calling the dispatch line.

Contact

Norwalk Water Damage Pros, 56 Stower Lane, Norwalk, OH 44857. Questions can be sent to contact@norwalkwaterdamagepros.com.

Our service area around Norwalk

Norwalk sits at the center of the service area, with Collins, Monroeville, Milan and Willard each within about 15 miles.
