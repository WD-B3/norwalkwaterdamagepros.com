Kitchen Water Damage in Norwalk, OH

Cabinets, countertops and the floor beneath them soak water quickly. Here is how kitchen water damage is dried and rebuilt.

☎ (567) 406-9153See all services

Kitchens combine supply lines, drains, a dishwasher and often a refrigerator with an ice maker, so water problems here are common. The damage shows up as swollen cabinet bases, a soft floor near the sink, a musty smell under the counter or a stain on the ceiling of the room below.

This page is about the kitchen as a room: cabinets, countertops, the sink area and the flooring. The appliance as a source is covered in appliance leak water damage.

Water under the sink

Under-sink leaks come from supply connections, the drain trap, a failed garbage disposal seal or a faucet base. Slow drips are the most damaging because they run for months. Particleboard cabinet bases swell and fall apart when wet, and the cabinet floor may be soaked long before anyone looks.

  • Open the cabinet and check the base for swelling or white powdery stains
  • Look at supply line connections for mineral crust or drips
  • Press the floor near the sink, since a soft spot means subfloor damage
  • Check the ceiling below for stains if the kitchen is on an upper floor

Technicians use moisture meters to check the cabinet base, toe-kick, wall behind and subfloor. Removing the toe-kick and drilling small holes allows air movers to dry hidden cavities. A dehumidifier works with the air movers to bring humidity down.

Rebuild decisions

Cabinets that are only wet at the base can sometimes be dried in place, while particleboard boxes usually have to be replaced. If you plan to remodel anyway, a water loss is a good time to decide, since the countertop must come off for cabinet replacement. Matching flooring across a kitchen can be a challenge, so ask your contractor early.

Keep the plumber's invoice and photos of the damage, since both help with insurance. Floors that run into other rooms may involve hardwood floor repair or carpet restoration.

Water behind cabinets hides well, so a moisture reading is worth it.

A Norwalk plumbing note

Norwalk's water and sewer service is run by the city, with billing handled at City Hall. Leaks inside the house, including those under a sink, are the owner's to repair. If your bill shows an unexplained jump, a check of your fixtures and kitchen supply lines is a good first step, followed by a call to the utility office.

The practical angle is to turn off the shutoff valves under the sink if you will be away, since a failed line can run for days when nobody is home. A leak that has run long can also point to a burst pipe in the wall behind the sink.

What affects kitchen water damage cost

Kitchens combine expensive materials in a small space:

  • Cabinet material and how many boxes were wet
  • Countertop removal and reinstall
  • Flooring type and area
  • Drywall behind the cabinets
  • Days of drying
  • Rebuild scope and any remodel decisions

A sudden failure, such as a burst supply line, is often covered by homeowners policies, while a slow drip that ran for months often is not. Check your policy or ask your insurer. Want your real number? Call dispatch.

Cabinet kick plates and hidden spots

Water often tracks under cabinets and behind the kick plate, so a kitchen can be wetter than it looks. Ask for readings at the toe kicks and under the sink base.

Kitchen Water Damage questions in Norwalk, OH

Can wet kitchen cabinets be saved?

Solid wood cabinets often can, if dried quickly. Particleboard boxes swell and usually need replacing.

How do I know if the subfloor under my sink is wet?

A soft spot, a musty smell or a stain below are signs. A moisture meter gives a firm answer without removing the floor.

Should I remove the countertop to dry cabinets?

Usually only if the cabinets themselves must be replaced. Many jobs can dry the base cavity with the countertop in place.
