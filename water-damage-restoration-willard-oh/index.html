Seasons and water in Willard

In winter the main risk is frozen pipes in garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls. In spring, snowmelt and heavy rain test sump pumps and foundations, and in summer a clogged air conditioner drain line can drip into a ceiling. Each season has its own page: frozen pipes in cold snaps, basement water in wet weather and AC leaks in humid stretches.

The practical step is to match the checklist to the season. Insulate exposed lines before the first hard freeze, test the sump pump in March and flush the air conditioner drain before July.

If water is still spreading: what to do first

Stop the source

Close the main valve, then open the lowest faucet to drain the lines.

Protect power

Switch off power only to wet areas, and only if the panel is dry.

Move belongings

Lift furniture, boxes and electronics onto dry surfaces.

Keep people clear

Keep kids and animals away from drain water.

A rail city and its buildings

Willard grew around a railroad junction and is home to a CSX rail yard. It was founded in 1874, incorporated in 1882 as Chicago Junction and renamed Willard in 1917.

Homes and businesses near the tracks and the older center of town may have older plumbing. Check the age of your own house, since a tired supply line is a common source of a sudden loss. Commercial water damage applies to shops and workplaces, and burst pipe damage to a split supply line.

The city's website lists code enforcement and zoning, so ask that office whether a rebuild needs a permit. Crews serving Monroeville to the west and Collins to the east answer through the same dispatch line.
